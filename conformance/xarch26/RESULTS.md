Two portability defects the kit found on the RTX 4090 before any digest could be taken, both host-side, both fixed in
t16m_kernels.cu and re-gated on the H100 (fused kernel gate 1,186 cases; per-token identity on Qwen3-8B and Phi-4-mini):
  1. the three 1,024-thread rotate kernels (r4quant_k, deqsilur4_k, r4_cl_k) had no launch bound; on sm_89 the compiler's
     register allocation exceeded the 64 per thread a 1,024-thread block allows and the launch was refused (invalid argument);
     the H100 build happened to fit.  Fix: __launch_bounds__(R4_NW * 32).
  2. the staged forms and the small-M GEMV asked cudaFuncSetAttribute for 200 KB of dynamic shared memory; the H100 grants it
     (227 KB opt-in), Ada (99 KB) and the PRO 6000 refuse it, and the refused call leaves a sticky cudaErrorInvalidValue that
     surfaces at the next launch.  Fix: request min(want, opt-in - the kernel's static shared memory), clear the error, and
     make each form eligible only within that allowance (smem_optin_cap / dyn_smem_allow / set_dyn_smem).
Two RTX 5090 rentals (secure cloud, stock "Low") never started their container (uptime 0 after 25 and 10 minutes) and were
deleted; the RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell (same sm_120 family) came up in under a minute.  The kit's t16l section also needs
t16k_blake3.cu alongside (the commitment hash is #included), and the outlier form is only defined with the rotation on.
